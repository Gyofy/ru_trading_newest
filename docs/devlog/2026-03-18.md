## Frozen OOS Validation Framework

Defined in `frozen_params_v4_1.yaml` under `frozen_oos` key:
- Method: walk-forward, 4 windows
- Train: 360 bars (60d), Test: 90 bars (15d), Gap: 6 bars (1d)
- Pass criteria:
  - min net EV/trade: +0.2%
  - min trades/window: 5
  - max avg DD: 5%
  - min positive windows: 3/4
- Output: `data/reports/frozen_oos_v4_1/`
